# Licensing decision pending (proposal)

The owner prefers a permissive license for GoHP, subject to dependency and
contributor audit. No final LICENSE is selected by this change. Until the owner
chooses and publishes terms, do not assume permission to redistribute this new
project as a licensed OSS release. No upstream implementation source is imported.

The initial Go module has no third-party Go dependencies. Official PHP and Go
container toolchains have their own upstream licenses/notices; do not erase them.
Upstream review records metadata and provenance, not a license to copy code.
Before reuse, inspect the exact revision, all transitive modules and notices,
generated-code/runtime obligations, SPDX identifiers and source distribution
requirements. WordPress GPLv2-or-later and every plugin/theme license need an
independent audit in Gossip. This document does not finalize legal conclusions.
